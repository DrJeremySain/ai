About My Work & Philosophy

I am a passionate educator and researcher currently working as a full-time Professor at Clarendon College. Over the past fifteen years, I have taught courses spanning from introductory Algebra to advanced differential equations, calculus, and linear algebra.

My core educational philosophy centers on *intuitive rigor*. I believe that while rigorous mathematical proof is the bedrock of logical science, students only truly grasp topics when they are allowed to visualize, manipulate, and explore systems hands-on.

Outside the classroom, I conduct pedagogical research, writing interactive math tools to simplify classic calculation blockages. My latest tool offers step-by-step visualizations for Partial Fraction Decomposition, rendering difficult integration techniques clear and modular.

Research Focus Areas

Investigating modern algebraic computation frameworks, abstract algebra structures, and high-efficiency pedagogical models for undergraduate mathematics.
