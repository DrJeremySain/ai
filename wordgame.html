The Math Behind the Game: This uses a Finite Projective Plane of order 17, known as PG(2, 17). It has exactly 307 points (our 3rd-grade sight words) and 307 lines.

Each "card" you see represents a line, containing exactly 18 points. By definition of a projective plane, any two lines intersect at exactly one point. Therefore, no matter which two distinct cards are randomly selected, there is mathematically guaranteed to be exactly one matching word!
