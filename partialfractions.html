This is comfortable! But what if we started on the right and had to find the left?

The Goal of Partial Fractions: We start with a heavy, complex fraction like $\frac{4x - 5}{x^2 - x - 2}$ and "decompose" (un-add) it back into simpler, "partial" fractions: $\frac{1}{x-2} + \frac{3}{x+1}$.

Why do we care?

In Calculus and engineering, handling a monster polynomial fraction as a single unit is incredibly painful. Breaking it down into separate baby fractions makes integration, limits, and modeling extremely easy! It's like taking a complex engine apart into simple, clean gears.

💡 Let's build an intuition: Denominator Factoring

To crack open the denominator, we must factor it first. Test your skills on this denominator. Factoring $x^2 + x - 6$ yields what two baby linear factors?

The Standard Setup (For Distinct Linear Factors)

Whenever your factored denominator has simple, distinct terms like $(x - a)(x - b)$, we assume there are some mystery constants (let's call them $A$ and $B$) sitting on top of them:

$$\frac{Px + Q}{(x-a)(x-b)} = \frac{A}{x-a} + \frac{B}{x-b}$$

Our entire mission is to solve for $A$ and $B$. Let's jump into the interactive lab next to see how we do that step-by-step with any numbers you choose!

Your Target Equation

$$\frac{3x + 1}{(x - 3)(x + 2)}$$

1 Set up the Equation

We assume that our target fraction can be written as a sum of two simple terms with mystery numerators $A$ and $B$:

$$\frac{3x + 1}{(x - 3)(x + 2)} = \frac{A}{x - 3} + \frac{B}{x + 2}$$

2 "Clear" the Denominators

Multiply the entire equation by the common denominator $(x - r_1)(x - r_2)$ to get rid of all fractions. Watch how the denominators cancel out:

$$3x + 1 = A(x + 2) + B(x - 3)$$

3 Solve for A (Eliminate B)

We want $B$ to disappear! To make $B(x - r_1) = 0$, let's intelligently choose x = 3:

Substitute: $3(3) + 1 = A(3 + 2) + B(3 - 3)$
Simplify: $10 = 5A \implies A = 2$

4 Solve for B (Eliminate A)

Now make $A$ disappear! To make $A(x - r_2) = 0$, let's intelligently choose x = -2:

Substitute: $3(-2) + 1 = A(-2 + 2) + B(-2 - 3)$
Simplify: $-5 = -5B \implies B = 1$

5 The Final Decomposed Output!

Assemble the pieces together with our calculated constants $A$ and $B$:

$$\frac{3x + 1}{(x - 3)(x + 2)} = \frac{2}{x - 3} + \frac{1}{x + 2}$$
